Frame trends•October 28, 2026

Color is having a moment

Meet the soft citrus, clear olive, and warm honey tones making everyday frames feel anything but ordinary.

Start with a shade you already reach for in your wardrobe. A translucent frame adds color without feeling loud, while a deeper tone can make a familiar silhouette feel new.

Styling•October 25, 2026

Find your frame, not a rule

A more personal way to think about face shape, proportion, and choosing glasses that feel right on you.

Use traditional face-shape guides as a starting point, not a checklist. Pay attention to fit, bridge comfort, and the proportions that make you feel most like yourself.

Lens guides•October 22, 2026

Blue light lenses, plainly explained

What they do, what they do not, and the practical things that can make long screen days easier on your eyes.

Evidence for special blue-light coatings easing digital eye strain is limited. Regular breaks, comfortable screen distance, and an up-to-date prescription are useful first steps.

Eye care•October 19, 2026

A gentler rhythm for screen days

Simple, sensible ways to rest your eyes through a busy day, from better lighting to regular moments away.

Try the 20-20-20 habit: about every 20 minutes, look at something around 20 feet away for 20 seconds. If discomfort persists, check in with an eye-care professional.

Lens guides•October 16, 2026

The lens upgrades worth knowing

Anti-reflective, photochromic, polarized: a clear guide to the lens features that suit different days.

Anti-reflective coatings reduce reflections, photochromic lenses darken outdoors, and polarized lenses cut reflected glare. The best choice depends on where and how you wear your glasses.

Styling•October 12, 2026

One frame, five ways

Build a small rotation around the glasses you already love, with easy outfit pairings for every kind of day.

Let the frame set the tone: pair a bold shape with clean lines, soften angular styles with knit textures, or repeat one frame color in a small accessory.
